 NeuroFit Assessment FAQ Knowledge Base

## General Information

### What is NeuroFit?
NeuroFit is a platform that provides comprehensive personality, cognitive, and cultural fit assessments to help companies find their perfect candidates. Our AI-powered assessment tools enable faster, fairer hiring that works.

### What problem does NeuroFit solve?
NeuroFit addresses three key problems in traditional hiring:
1. Manual Screening Overload: HR teams spend 23 hours per role manually sifting through resumes, leading to fatigue-based decisions.
2. Culture Mis-Hires: 87% of failed placements stem from cultural misalignment, not skill gaps.
3. Hidden Cognitive Gaps: Traditional interviews miss the cognitive abilities that predict 40% of job success.

### How does NeuroFit work?
NeuroFit uses a three-step approach:
1. Automated pre-screening saves HR teams per role
2. Value-matching algorithms reduce culture mis-hires by 77%
3. Cognitive testing predicts performance with 6.4x higher accuracy

## Assessments

### What types of assessments does NeuroFit offer?
NeuroFit offers three core assessments:
1. Personality Test: Evidence-based trait assessment that provides comprehensive evaluation, mobile-friendly interface, and validated against 500,000 profiles.
2. Cognitive Aptitude: Adaptive numerical & logical tests that are skills-based, not knowledge-based, and accurately measure job-relevant attributes.
3. Cultural-Fit Builder: Customizable values assessment with HR-defined company values, questions about expectations, and batch access to test and hire.

### How accurate are NeuroFit's assessments?
Our cognitive testing predicts performance with 6.4x higher accuracy compared to traditional interviews, and our value-matching algorithms reduce culture mis-hires by 77%.

### Are NeuroFit assessments mobile-friendly?
Yes, all our assessments, including the personality test, feature a mobile-friendly interface.

## AI Role-Fit Analysis

### What is the AI Role-Fit Analysis?
Our GPT-powered system analyzes assessment results against role requirements, creating a detailed role-fit report. Each candidate receives a dynamic fit score, and hiring teams get an email summary with action recommendations.

### How does the Role-Fit Analysis help hiring decisions?
The AI Role-Fit Analysis provides a comprehensive breakdown of how well a candidate matches a specific role based on their assessment results. It helps hiring teams make data-driven decisions and provides clear action recommendations.
